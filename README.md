# Hospital-Department-Volumes-
This pro-folio analyzes hospital department volumes . The goal of this profolio is to analyze and investigate the main cause of high level department visit volumes , by month, day of the week. By the 12 months we see theres a unexpected change through out the early months from January to the middle of summer. The struggles hospitals face everyday from complicated delays, low number of staffs, and coverage. This profolio helps to find the cause of increased visits through each department, and what the solution can help to reduce stress ,complications, and save time.
